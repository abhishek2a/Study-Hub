Exam Day Tips

  • Read requirements carefully before jumping to the case background.
  • Show all workings clearly in Section C (partial marks are lifesavers).
  • Manage time wisely: strictly abide by the 1.8 minutes per mark rule.
  • Use correct IFRS terminology: cite standard titles where relevant.
  • Never leave any question blank: attempt every OTQ!

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Reading text without deep practical understanding of standards.
  • Skipping presentation standards or theoretical Framework concepts.
  • Ignoring Section C daily timed drafting practice until the final week.
  • Not practicing under strict 3-hour CBE time constraints.
  • Not analyzing and revising recurring calculation errors.

2. Complete Syllabus Modular Guide

A. Context & Purpose
  • Regulatory & Conceptual Framework for financial reporting
  • Qualitative characteristics of useful financial information
  • Ethics and professional accounting principles

  • Statement of Financial Position (SFP) drafting
  • Statement of Profit or Loss & Other Comprehensive Income
  • Statement of Changes in Equity & Statement of Cash Flows
C. Recognition & Measurement
  • Tangible vs Intangible non-current asset valuation
  • Measurement bases, depreciation, amortization & impairment
  • Revenue recognition under contracts with customers
D. Additional Disclosures
  • Financial Instruments accounting and impairment (IFRS 9)
  • Events after the Reporting Period (IAS 10) & Provisions (IAS 37)
  • Related party disclosures and reporting rules
E. Accounting Techniques
  • Accounting for Leases by Lessees and Lessors (IFRS 16)
  • Group Consolidation (IFRS 10, 3 & IAS 28 Associates)
  • Foreign currency transactions and monetary items (IAS 21)
F. Analysis & Interpretation
  • Profitability, liquidity, efficiency and gearing ratio calculation
  • Evaluating financial health and reporting limitations
  • Interpreting consolidated financial performance

Quick revision formula guide and pro-forma calculations for all 19 core Financial Reporting standards and topics.

1. Accounting Equation & Framework
Basic Accounting Equation:
Assets = Liabilities + Equity
Equity (Net Assets):
Equity = Total Assets - Total Liabilities
Closing Net Assets:
Opening Equity + Profit - Drawings + New Capital
2 & 3. Working Capital & Liquidity
Net Working Capital:
Current Assets - Current Liabilities
Current Ratio:
Current Assets / Current Liabilities (X : 1)
Quick (Acid Test) Ratio:
(Current Assets - Inventories) / Current Liabilities
4. Inventory Valuation (IAS 2)
Core Valuation Rule:
Lower of Cost and Net Realisable Value (NRV)
Net Realisable Value (NRV):
Est. Selling Price - Completion Costs - Selling Costs
Weighted Average Cost (AVCO):
Total Cost of Goods Avail. / Total Units Avail.
5. PP&E & Revaluation (IAS 16)
Straight-Line Depreciation:
(Cost - Residual Value) / Useful Life
Carrying Amount:
Cost - Accumulated Depr. - Impairment Losses
Revaluation Surplus (OCI / Equity):
Fair Value (Revalued Amount) - Carrying Amount
6 & 7. Intangibles & Impairment (IAS 38/36)
Recoverable Amount (IAS 36):
Higher of (FV less Disposal Costs) & (Value in Use)
Impairment Loss Calculation:
Carrying Amount - Recoverable Amount
Amortisation (Finite Life IAS 38):
(Cost - Residual Value) / Useful Life
8. Financial Instruments (IFRS 9)
Amortised Cost Carrying Balance:
Opening Balance + Effective Interest - Cash Coupon
Finance Cost (P&L Expense/Income):
Opening Carrying Amount × Effective Interest Rate
Cash Coupon Paid / Received:
Nominal Principal Amount × Nominal (Coupon) Rate %
9. Leases & ROU Asset (IFRS 16)
Initial ROU Asset Measurement:
Initial Liability + Direct Costs + Prepayments - Incentives
Subsequent Lease Liability:
Opening Liability + (Opening × Rate) - Lease Payment
Lease Liability Initial Value:
Present Value (PV) of unpaid future lease payments
10. Revenue Recognition (IFRS 15)
5-Step Recognition Model:
1. Contract | 2. Obligations | 3. Price | 4. Allocate | 5. Recognise
Transaction Price Allocation:
(Standalone Price / Total Standalone Prices) × Total Tx Price
Recognition Timing Rule:
Recognise as or when each obligation is satisfied
11 & 12. Foreign Currency & Provisions
Monetary Items (Receivables/Payables):
Retranslate at Closing Rate (Exch. gains/losses to P&L)
Non-Monetary Items (PPE/Inventory):
Do NOT retranslate at year-end (keep at historic spot rate)
Provision Recognition Rule (IAS 37):
Present Obligation + Probable Outflow (>50%) + Reliable Estimate
13 & 14. Taxation (IAS 12) & EPS (IAS 33)
Deferred Tax Asset / Liability:
Temporary Difference (Carrying Amount - Tax Base) × Tax Rate %
Basic EPS (Earnings Per Share):
Profit Attrib. to Ordinary Shares / Weighted Avg Shares
Total P&L Tax Expense:
Current Tax Payable ± Movement in Deferred Tax Balance
15 & 16. Cash Flow & Consolidation
Goodwill on Acquisition (IFRS 3):
Consideration + NCI at Acquisition - Fair Value Net Identifiable Assets
NCI at Reporting Date (IFRS 10):
NCI at Acquisition + NCI Share of Post-Acquisition Reserves
Operating Cash Flow (IAS 7):
PBIT + Depr/Amort ± Working Capital Movements - Tax & Interest Paid
17 - 19. Ratio Analysis (Performance Evaluation) & Key Abbreviations
ROCE (Return on Capital Employed):
(PBIT / Capital Employed) × 100%
Asset Turnover:
Revenue / Capital Employed (Expressed as X times)
Gross Profit Margin:
(Gross Profit / Revenue) × 100%
Operating Profit Margin (PBIT Margin):
(PBIT / Revenue) × 100%
Gearing Ratio (Debt / Equity):
(Non-Current Liabilities / Total Equity) × 100%
Interest Coverage Ratio:
PBIT / Finance Costs (Expressed as X times)
Inventory Turnover & Days:
(Average Inventory / Cost of Sales) × 365 days
Receivables Collection Days:
(Trade Receivables / Revenue) × 365 days
Payables Payment Days:
(Trade Payables / Cost of Sales or Purchases) × 365 days
Capital Employed Definition:
Total Equity + Non-Current Liabilities (or Total Assets - Current Liabilities)
Key FR Abbreviations: ROU = Right-of-Use Asset | PV = Present Value | EIR = Effective Interest Rate | NCI = Non-Controlling Interest | FV = Fair Value | NRV = Net Realisable Value | EPS = Earnings Per Share | PBIT = Profit Before Interest & Tax | ROCE = Return on Capital Employed | OCI = Other Comprehensive Income
